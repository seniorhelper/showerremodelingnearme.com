Three costs that surprise people

What's behind the wall

Soft drywall, rot, or mold behind an old surround has to be fixed before anything new goes up. Ask how the bid handles it: a fixed allowance, or priced after demo.

The valve

Reusing the old valve saves money, but an older single-handle valve may not be pressure-balancing. Replacing it means a plumber and often a permit.

Glass

A frameless glass door or panel can add a large chunk to the bill. A curtain rod or a framed door keeps the same shower far cheaper.

Get a real price faster: measure first

  1. Floor opening. Width wall to wall, and depth from the back wall to the front of the tub or curb.
  2. Wall height. From the tub rim or shower floor to the ceiling, or to where the old tile stops.
  3. Drain side. Left, right, or center as you face it.
  4. Two photos. One straight on, one from the doorway, showing valve, window, and floor.

With those four things, an installer can usually give you a ballpark on the phone instead of booking a sales visit.

Shower remodel questions

How much does a shower remodel cost?

Published 2026 guides range widely: about $1,500 to $20,000 for a shower replacement. Basic premade kits run about $1,500 to $3,000, custom walk-in showers about $4,000 to $20,000, and tub-to-shower conversions about $8,000 to $15,000 because the plumbing and floor change.

Is a tile or acrylic shower better?

Tile gives the most design choice and suits curbless showers, but needs a properly built waterproof layer and grout care. Acrylic and other panel systems install faster, have few or no grout lines, and are easier to clean, with fewer style choices.

How long does a shower remodel take?

Panel and acrylic systems are often installed in one to two days once materials arrive. A tiled shower usually takes one to three weeks because of waterproofing, curing, tile and grout steps, plus any inspection.

Can you put new shower walls over old tile?

Some panel systems can go over sound, flat tile. If the tile is loose, cracked, or there is any sign of water behind it, the old wall must come out so the damage can be fixed first.
